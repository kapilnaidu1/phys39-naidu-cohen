Phys 39 Module 3, Part 3: cooling direction, crossing below ambient
Naidu / Cohen, 23 September 2026

Sketch on the board: an older copy of tec_manual_hardware_direction with
PIN9_IS_HEAT = true (header printed "PWM on pin 9 = HEAT", and the bracketed
diagnostic has no "pot ADC" field). The printed heating/cooling word is
therefore WRONG in this log. The physical facts are the pin 11 level, the
named PWM pin, and the temperature column, and those are what is used below.

Supply: 12 V on at the H-bridge.
Ambient: 21.47 C, held flat for 45 s at the start and again from t = 53 to 90.

RESULT

    pin 11 = 5 V  ->  PWM on pin 9, duty 28 to 29  ->  plate FELL to 2.85 C
    BELOW ambient and was still falling.

    Therefore pin 9 is the COOLING direction and PIN9_IS_HEAT = false.

    This independently confirms the value committed on 16 September from a
    different pair of runs, and it resolves the contradiction logged in
    part3_direction_calibration.txt, where one later run appeared to show
    pin 9 heating. That run is superseded: it was judged on about 3 s of
    drive, which this log shows is far shorter than the onset delay.

THE ONSET DELAY, WHICH MATTERS FOR EVERY FUTURE RUN

    Duty 28 was commanded at t = 90.0 and the temperature did not move at
    all until t = 125.0. That is a 35 SECOND DELAY between commanding drive
    and seeing any thermal response.

    Consequence: a drive interval shorter than about 45 s carries no
    information about direction. Several earlier attempts today and last
    week were 3 to 7 s long and were read as "the TEC is doing nothing".
    They were simply too short.

NUMBERS

    ambient                    21.47 C
    duty commanded             28 to 29 of 255, on pin 9
    drive begins               t = 90.0 s
    first temperature motion   t = 125.0 s        (35 s onset delay)
    crosses below ambient       t = about 126 s
    at t = 180.5               18.62 C, still falling
    fall rate                  (18.62 - 21.47) / (180.5 - 124.5)
                               = -0.051 C/s = -3.1 C/min

    Near the crossing the plate sat at ambient, where passive decay
    contributes nothing, and it still moved at 0.051 C/s. By t = 180.5 it
    was 2.85 C below ambient, where passive heat gain is pushing the plate
    UP at about 2.85 / 146 = 0.020 C/s, so the Peltier was removing about
    0.051 + 0.020 = 0.071 C/s worth against the room.

    Passive physics can only move the plate toward room temperature. A plate
    driven below ambient and held there is active cooling and nothing else.

EARLIER IN THE SAME LOG, AND WHY IT PROVES NOTHING

    t = 45.5 to 53.0 the trim pot was intermittent and duty bounced
    2, 0, 3, 1, 2, 0, 0, 0, 12, 64, 64, 64, 64, 64, 64, 23, 0.
    Duty 64 was held for about 3 s. Temperature: unchanged at 21.47 C.

    With a 35 s onset delay that null is expected and carries no
    information. It was initially misread as evidence that the 12 V supply
    was not reaching the bridge. It was not: the supply was on, the interval
    was too short.

TRIM POT

    Still intermittent. Duty wandered 28 to 30 across the run without the
    knob being touched, and jumped erratically between t = 45 and 53. The
    run is usable because the temperature trace is continuous and the duty
    stayed within 28 to 30 for the whole measured interval, but the pot
    needs its contact fixed before a submitted run.

TEMPERATURE CHANNEL

    A0 was excellent throughout: 558.0 counts held to the fourth digit for
    the entire 90 s baseline, no dropouts, no excursions. Every R field
    agrees with the beta model to better than 0.03 kOhm, spot-checked at
    21.47 C (120.00 kOhm) and 18.62 C (139.49 kOhm).

STILL OUTSTANDING

    The heating half. Flip pin 11 to 0 V, drive pin 10 at the same duty, and
    hold for AT LEAST 90 s given the onset delay. Expect the plate to rise
    away from 21.47 C. That closes the calibration in both directions.
