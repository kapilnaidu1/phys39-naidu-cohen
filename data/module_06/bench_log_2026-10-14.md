# Bench log, 14 October 2026

## Why

The instructor asked for the high-gain P and PI runs to be pushed much
harder, into actuator saturation. On 13 October the Kp = 64 run reached
+/-255 for only about 2 s and the Ki = 1.0 run never did. Runs 3 and 6 were
repeated at higher gain; everything else unchanged (sketch
`arduino/module_06/tec_pi_safety`, 1000-sample averaging, setpoints 30, 35,
30 C, PWM limit 255, anti-windup on). The GUI's gain boxes were widened to
Kp 500 and Ki 50 before the runs; the control law was not changed.

## Files

- `pi_run_20261014_111300.csv`: header only. The GUI received no readings
  because the Arduino IDE still held the serial port. Closed, IDE quit,
  relaunched.
- `pi_run_20261014_111720.csv`: both runs.

## Run 3, P, Kp = 200 (38 s to 406 s)

Started from the plate at room temperature, 21.9 C. Stages: 30 C for 125 s,
35 C for 119 s, 30 C for 124 s. The temperature oscillated without decaying
in every stage, about 1.8 to 2.6 C peak to peak with a 6.2 to 6.3 s period,
and the command swung across most of the +/-255 range.

## Run 6, PI, Kp = 12, Ki = 20 (461 s to 1092 s)

- Control was switched on at 461 s with Ki still 0; Ki = 20 was entered at
  476 s. The integral had reached 38 C s, so u_I jumped to 760 and the first
  cycle peaked at 42.52 C, the highest temperature of the session.
- 30 C until 653 s, 35 C until 858 s.
- Control was off from 858.7 s to 883.5 s (about 25 s; the setpoint was
  changed back to 30 C during this time). Turned back on without zeroing the
  integral.
- 30 C from 885.8 s to 1092 s.
- The oscillation grew to about 10.6 to 10.9 C peak to peak with an 18 to
  19 s period and the command reaching +/-255 every half cycle, then stayed
  that size.

The plate stayed below the 50 C stop limit throughout; the Arduino's 60 C
latch never tripped.
