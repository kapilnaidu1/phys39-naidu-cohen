The foam-off hold had only about one minute at the setpoint and was still
falling, so the 6-count difference is not a measurement of the foam effect.
A settled foam-off hold (6 to 8 min at 50 °C) is needed. The settled foam-on
value is the usable number from this run.

**Comparison.** Foam off needed 68.6 counts (10 to 70 s after first reaching
49.9 °C, still falling about 0.5 counts/min); foam on settled at 62.5. On 19
October the foam-off 45 °C hold, measured at the same 10 to 70 s point, was only
0.7 % above its settled value. If the same holds here, foam off would settle near
68, about 9 % above foam on. This single-setpoint comparison cannot separate a
change in H from a warm-up shift of the effective room temperature (water loop
and room warming during the session): a 2.8 °C shift would explain all 6 counts.
On both days the first, foam-off block needed more power, which fits warm-up
drift. The 19 October two-setpoint slope, which cancels such offsets, gave
H(off)/H(on) = 1.000 ± 0.010. To decide, repeat with on, off, on bracketing at
two setpoints.
