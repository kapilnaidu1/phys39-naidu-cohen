========================================================================
PART 7   Integral action, and what it costs
========================================================================

  Baseline P-only at Kp = 2 (L = 1.003): droop 4.225 C, no overshoot by construction.
  Underdamped when (1+L)^2 < 4*tau*chi*Ki, i.e. Ki > 0.0318 counts/(C s).

       Ki    zeta       damping  final droop  overshoot  settle to 0.1C
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
    0.002   3.987    overdamped       2.6725      0.000           never
    0.010   1.783    overdamped       0.3590      0.000           never
    0.050   0.797   underdamped      -0.0000      0.273           277 s
    0.200   0.399   underdamped       0.0000      2.374           271 s

  P-only settles to 0.1 C of setpoint: never, the droop is 4.22 C.
  Integral action removes the droop entirely. The cost is overshoot, which
  appears exactly where zeta drops below 1, and windup, which Part 8 covers.

  Windup. Holding 45 C needs 47 counts, and the bridge supplies 255, so
  the real apparatus never saturates for long at a safe setpoint and never winds up. The
  panel below imposes a 50-count limit, as Part 8 asks us to suppose.

                  peak T / C  overshoot  peak integral  clamped for
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
        plain PI       46.46      +1.46          1204         313 s
     anti-windup       45.26      +0.26           967          22 s
